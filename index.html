01 · The Firm

Who We Are

Mount Royal Solutions, LLC is a Virginia limited liability company based in Alexandria, Virginia. The firm works in five disciplines — advisory services, project and program management, strategy consulting, procurement and acquisition support, and international logistics — in one region: Africa, with particular depth in West Africa.

West Africa rewards first-hand knowledge. Ports, customs regimes, ministries and local markets each carry their own practice and precedent. The firm's principals run commercial operations in the region — agriculture, trading, logistics — and bring that operating experience to every engagement.

The firm works in English and French, and documents its work to the standards of Government program offices and contracting officers — bridging U.S. management and compliance expectations with execution on the ground in Africa.

Mount Royal Solutions takes prime contracts suited to a small specialist firm and teams with established primes that need responsive regional execution: local vendor and stakeholder coordination, bilingual capability, and senior-level program management without permanent in-country overhead.

02 · Practice Areas

Key Disciplines

  • IAdvisory

    Advisory Services

    Country and market assessments, stakeholder and institutional analysis, operating-environment and risk reviews, commercial and program feasibility analysis, and engagement counsel for organizations entering or operating in African markets. The firm's advice is grounded in current, first-hand commercial experience in the region.

  • IIPrograms

    Project & Program Management

    Program planning, work-plan development, scheduling, budget tracking, milestone management, implementation oversight, quality control, field coordination, vendor and subcontractor management, and management reporting prepared to the standards of Government program offices.

  • IIIStrategy

    Strategy Consulting

    Program strategy, market-entry planning, operating-model development, capture support, organizational analysis, interagency and stakeholder coordination frameworks, and decision-support assessments intended to support defensible decisions in complex regional circumstances.

  • IVProcurement

    Procurement & Acquisition Support

    Market research, supplier identification and qualification, sourcing, solicitation and evaluation support, vendor coordination, purchase-order administration, procurement tracking, and contract-administration support for programs operating in Africa — transparent, documented procurement aligned with the requirements of Government agencies, prime contractors and institutional clients. Services are provided as contractor support and do not involve inherently governmental authority.

  • VLogistics

    International Logistics

    Freight-forwarding coordination, transportation planning, customs and port coordination, warehousing, shipment tracking, chain-of-custody documentation, distribution planning, and final-mile delivery into African markets — for equipment, vehicles and materiel.
