01 · The Firm

A professional services firm of deliberate focus

Mount Royal Solutions, LLC is a professional services firm organized in the Commonwealth of Virginia and based in Northern Virginia, within reach of the principal offices of its Government clientele. The firm confines its work to four disciplines — advisory services, project management, strategy consulting and international logistics — and to one region, Africa, with particular attention to West Africa.

This focus is intentional. The countries of West Africa present operating conditions in which general knowledge is of limited value: ports, customs regimes, ministries and local markets each carry their own practice and precedent. The firm's principals maintain active commercial operations in the region, in agriculture, trading and logistics, and bring to each engagement the judgment that comes from conducting one's own affairs there — not merely from studying those of others.

The firm works in English and French, the two administrative languages of the region it serves. Engagements are staffed by principals, conducted with discretion, and documented to the standards expected by Government program offices and contracting officers.

Mount Royal Solutions accepts engagements as a prime contractor on matters suited to a specialist firm, as a subcontractor or teaming partner to established prime contractors, and in an advisory capacity to organizations operating alongside United States Government programs in Africa.

02 · Practice Areas

Four disciplines, practiced with restraint

  • IAdvisory

    Advisory Services

    Country and market assessments, stakeholder and institutional analysis, risk and political-economy review, and engagement counsel for programs entering or operating in West African environments. The firm's advice is grounded in current, first-hand commercial experience in the region.

  • IIProjects

    Project Management

    Management of projects and task orders through their full life cycle: planning and scheduling, budget and quality control, vendor and subcontractor oversight, and reporting prepared to the standards of Government program offices.

  • IIIStrategy

    Strategy Consulting

    Program strategy, market-entry and capture support, interagency coordination frameworks, and written assessments intended to support defensible decisions in complex regional circumstances.

  • IVLogistics

    International Logistics

    Coordination of freight forwarding, customs and port operations, warehousing and final-mile delivery into West Africa, with documented chain of custody for equipment, vehicles and materiel.
