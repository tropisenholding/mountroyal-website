01 · The Firm

Who We Are

Mount Royal Solutions, LLC is a Virginia limited liability company based in Alexandria, Virginia. The firm works in five disciplines — advisory services, project and program management, strategy consulting, procurement and acquisition support, and international logistics — with Africa as its core market and particular depth in West Africa.

West Africa rewards first-hand knowledge. Ports, customs regimes, ministries and local markets each carry their own practice and precedent. The firm's leadership has run agribusiness, mining, trading and logistics operations in the region since 2010, and brings that operating experience to every engagement.

The firm works in English, French, Portuguese, Spanish and Russian — across Anglophone, Francophone and Lusophone Africa and beyond — and documents its work to the standards of Government program offices and contracting officers, bridging U.S. management and compliance expectations with execution on the ground.

Mount Royal Solutions takes prime contracts suited to a small specialist firm and teams with established primes that need responsive regional execution: local vendor and stakeholder coordination, bilingual capability, and senior-level program management without permanent in-country overhead.

08 · Compliance & Integrity

Standards We Hold

  • Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption

    Zero tolerance for bribery and no facilitation payments; full compliance with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and host-country law.

  • Anti-Money Laundering & KYC

    Know-your-customer and know-your-supplier checks, including beneficial ownership; payments only through regulated banks to verified accounts.

  • Sanctions & Export Controls

    Every client, supplier, subcontractor and payee screened against OFAC and other U.S. restricted-party lists, with UN and EU lists where relevant.

  • Federal Procurement Integrity

    Adherence to the Procurement Integrity Act, the Anti-Kickback Act and FAR contingent-fee rules; conflicts of interest identified and disclosed.

  • Records, Audit & Chain of Custody

    Complete documentation of every purchase, payment and delivery; audit-ready books and no off-book arrangements.

  • Safeguarding & Flow-Down

    Federal contract information protected under FAR 52.204-21 basic controls; obligations flowed down to every subcontractor and supplier.
