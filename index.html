01 · The Firm

Who We Are

Mount Royal Solutions, LLC is a Virginia limited liability company headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia. The firm operates across five core disciplines — advisory services, project and program management, strategy consulting, procurement and acquisition support, and international logistics. Africa is the firm's primary operational focus, with its personnel, resources and field expertise concentrated on the continent, particularly in West Africa. This foundation is complemented by country-specific knowledge and sector expertise in Eastern Europe and in Southeast Asia, extending the firm's geographic reach and cross-border capabilities.

Successful operations in Africa require more than sound strategy; they demand first-hand knowledge, established relationships and an understanding of local realities. Ports, customs regimes, government institutions and commercial markets operate according to distinct practices and precedents. Since 2010, the firm's leadership has managed agribusiness, mining, trading and logistics operations across West Africa, developing the practical experience, regional networks and operational judgment necessary to navigate challenging environments and deliver results.

The firm operates in English, French, Portuguese, Spanish and Russian, supporting engagements across Anglophone, Francophone and Lusophone Africa and beyond. Mount Royal Solutions combines regional and sector-specific knowledge with U.S. management, reporting and compliance expectations, documenting its work to the standards required by government program offices and contracting officers while bridging the gap between strategic objectives and execution on the ground.

Mount Royal Solutions pursues prime contracts suited to a specialized, agile firm and partners with established prime contractors requiring dependable regional execution. Its Africa-focused team provides local vendor and stakeholder coordination, multilingual engagement, procurement and logistics support, and senior-level program management without requiring clients to maintain permanent in-country infrastructure.

06 · Compliance & Federal Contracting

Standards We Hold

  • Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption

    Bribery and facilitation payments are prohibited; work is conducted in accordance with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and applicable host-country law.

  • Anti-Money Laundering & KYC

    Know-your-customer and know-your-supplier due diligence, including beneficial ownership where relevant; payments made through regulated banking channels to verified accounts.

  • Sanctions & Export Controls

    Clients, suppliers, subcontractors and payees are screened against OFAC and other U.S. restricted-party lists, and against UN and EU lists where relevant.

  • Federal Procurement Integrity

    Adherence to the Procurement Integrity Act, the Anti-Kickback Act and FAR contingent-fee rules; organizational conflicts of interest are identified and disclosed.

  • Records, Audit & Chain of Custody

    Purchases, payments and deliveries are documented and records kept audit-ready, with no off-book arrangements.

  • Safeguarding & Flow-Down

    Federal contract information safeguarded in line with FAR 52.204-21 basic controls; compliance obligations flowed down to subcontractors and suppliers.
